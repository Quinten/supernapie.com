**Message for a future mountaineer**

While climbing, a mountaineer discovers some runes that speak of a past civilisation.

Made for [Weekly Game Jam 146](https://itch.io/jam/weekly-game-jam-146).

Theme: Mountain Climber

### Controls

Run: left and right cursors

Jump: space

Climb: all 4 cursors (up, down, left and right)

Gamepads should also work...
