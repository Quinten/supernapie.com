**Always make sure to add your company logo to your web games, because you never know where it will popup next.**

If you have been in the web games business for some time and you googled your own games, you might have noticed that your games start to appear on some obscure sites, where you didn't upload them yourself. Pirate sites make easy money of your games.

Let them have the pennies, while you build up your good name and get free advertisement, by adding your logo and other credits all over your game.

But beware some sjmerks will try to remove them or even replace them, so try to make it as hard as possible for them to do that.
