**A puzzle platformer about two brothers who are thick as thieves.**

Originally cobbled up in 72 hours for Ludum Dare 44. Theme: Your life is currency

Use keyboard or gamepad or both.

Instructions are in the game.

Thanks to everyone who voted Nutty Twins into an 11th place in the innovation category!

Now that the voting is over, i have updated the game with a (work in progress) version that has new levels and lots of tackled feedback in it.

I am hoping to get the level count up to 18 in the next month.

Find out more in the [post mortem](https://supernapie.com/blog/ludum-dare-44-post-mortem).
