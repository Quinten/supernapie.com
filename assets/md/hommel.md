**A short game where you have to survive as a bumble bee**

Made in 48 hours for Ludum Dare 46. Theme: Keep it alive

Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move left and right.

Hold down space to fly.

Gamepad also works!

If you submitted a game for this Ludum Dare game jam, you are welcome to vote on [my submission page](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/46/hommel) and if you leave a comment there i will play and rate yours.
