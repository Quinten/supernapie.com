**Escape with invisibility super powers**

Distract the blue bullies with your invisibility super power and escape a nerve-racking game.

Made for [Weekly Game Jam 147](https://itch.io/jam/weekly-game-jam-147).

Theme: Invisible

### Controls

Run: left and right cursors

Jump: space / a

Invisibility: hold down x

Gamepads should also work...
