A downloadable game for Windows, macOS and Linux

## a crash into the obscure

**Jimmy wakes up next to his wrecked Porsche 550. He is all alone in a haunted pine forest. He tries to find help, but stumbles deeper into an outlandish conspiracy...**

Esciit is a surreal adventure platformer.

### Esciit is in early access

At this moment the game is playable, but the story ends with a cliffhanger.  There is still more story to be added. The first part took 3 months to make and the second part of the story will probably take about the same time. The second part will be released as an update to this game.

Download this game on [itch.io](https://supernapie.itch.io/esciit).
