**Obviously i don't know anything about soccer. I only heard 'soccer is war'.**

Soccer Slasher is a sports hack 'n slash. You need to slay enemies by kicking a ball at them, before they can hit you.

### How to play

You control your player with the cursor keys. Tip: you can walk diagonally by pressing two cursors keys at once.

Currently there is only support for keyboard input.

### About the game

Soccer Slasher was made in two days for [Ludum Dare 41](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/41). So I kept it very simple. You can read more about what i learned from this experience [here](https://supernapie.com/blog/ludum-dare-41-post-mortem).

Maybe i will flesh it out just a little more later (with the feedback i got). Thats why i've put in the list with the other games.
